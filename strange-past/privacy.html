Strange Past Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Strange Past ("the app") is made by Two Oaks Digital. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and it does not use analytics or advertising tools.

Information stored on your device

Your card collection, streak and settings are saved only on your phone so the app remembers your progress. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Subscriptions

Strange Past+ is purchased through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive your payment details.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a card, your phone's share menu opens and you choose where the text goes. The app does not see or store what you share.

Deleting your data

Go to Profile → Reset progress, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the app stored.

Children

The app is meant for users 13 and older and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
