Strange Past Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 23, 2026 (auction house added)

Strange Past ("the app") is made by Two Oaks Digital. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information we collect

The app does not ask for your name, email, location or contacts, and it does not use analytics or advertising tools.

To run the auction house, the app gives you an anonymous account: a random ID with no name or email attached. When you list a card or place a bid, we store your anonymous ID, your collector name (a random name like "Collector 4821"), the card, the prices, your bids and the times. This is stored with Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and is used only to run auctions.

Other players can see your collector name, the cards you list and the bids you place. Your anonymous ID is stored alongside your listings and bids so the auction house can tell players apart; it is not linked to your name, email or phone.

Information stored on your device

Your card collection, Relics, streak and settings are saved on your phone so the app remembers your progress. This data is not sent to us.

Subscriptions

Strange Past+ is purchased through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy, and we do not receive your payment details.

Sharing

If you tap Share on a card, your phone's share menu opens and you choose where the text goes. The app does not see or store what you share.

Deleting your data

Go to Profile → Reset progress, or uninstall the app, to remove everything stored on your phone. To delete your auction records, email us your collector name and we will remove them within 30 days. Records of finished auctions are otherwise kept for up to 12 months so disputes can be checked.

Children

The app is meant for users 13 and older and does not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
